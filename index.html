HOW IT WORKS

The game identifies the important characters, world, conflicts, symbols, themes and turning points.

02

PLAY THE STORY

You explore a world designed specifically around that book. Discoveries reveal the story through experience.

03

When you remember the book, remember the game.
When you remember the game, remember the book.

CICAN BOOK QUEST is designed around the connection between experience and knowledge. The game creates a mental map. The book fills that map with detail. Together, they make the story easier to understand — and harder to forget.
